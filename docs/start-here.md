# Zero to ROAS: the beginner guide

This guide takes you from nothing to your first useful result in four stages. You do not need to be technical. The kit is free and needs no paid ad tool. Claude Code itself needs a paid Claude plan.

ROAS means "return on ad spend": the money that comes back for each unit of money you spend on ads. It is the number this whole kit is trying to improve.

## Stage 1: install (5 minutes)

1. Install [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) if you do not have it.
2. Open Claude Code and paste these, one at a time:

   ```text
   /plugin marketplace add jawadbuilds/claude-for-paid-ads
   /plugin install paid-ads@jawadbuilds
   ```

3. Restart Claude Code.
4. Type: `help me with my ads`

Claude asks you three short questions and takes it from there.

Want the deep audit and SEO plugins too? See [the README](../README.md#install).

## Stage 2: give Claude your numbers (2 minutes with an export)

Pick one route. You can change later.

| Route | Time | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| [Export a report](use-exports.md) | 2 minutes | Everyone. Start here if unsure. |
| [Connect Meta](connect-meta.md) | 5 minutes | Facebook and Instagram advertisers |
| [Connect Google](connect-google.md) | An hour or more | People comfortable with technical setup |

Or just say: `connect my Meta ad account` and the setup guide walks you through it.

## Stage 3: check your tracking (10 minutes)

Before judging any result, make sure results are counted correctly. Say:

```text
check my tracking
```

You get six checks, each marked pass, fail or not checked, and the one fix to do first. If tracking is broken, fix it before spending more. Every decision after this depends on it.

## Stage 4: your first audit (10 minutes)

Say one of these:

```text
audit my Google Ads
audit my Meta ads
find my wasted spend
```

You get what is working, what is not, and what to fix first, with the numbers behind each point.

## What to do each week

| When | Say |
|---|---|
| Monday | `weekly report for my ads` |
| When results drop | `why did my cost per result go up` |
| When ads feel tired | `which of my ads are wearing out` |
| Before new ads | `what are my competitors running` |
| When copy reads like AI | `remove the AI slop from this ad` |
| Before any launch | `build this campaign, paused` |

## Three rules that keep you safe

1. Audits and reports never change your account.
2. Claude asks before changing anything. You see the exact change and say yes or no.
3. New campaigns are created paused. You switch them on.

More in [safety](safety.md).

## Stuck?

Say `I'm new, explain that simply` at any point. Claude switches to small steps and plain words.
